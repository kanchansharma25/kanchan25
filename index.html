About

Kanchan Sharma

Hi, I'm Kanchan.

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Data Science and Engineering at IISER Bhopal, supervised by Dr. Vaibhav Kumar. My research lies at the intersection of computer vision and urban planning, using visual and spatial data to understand urban environments and inform more effective planning decisions. I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics from IIT Kanpur and a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Mathematics from Raj Rishi Autonomous College, Alwar, Raj. Outside research, I play badminton, read, write, and travel.
