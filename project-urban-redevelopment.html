Overview

I work on understanding what makes an urban street feel safe, lively, and pleasant, and using that understanding to suggest realistic changes that could improve it.

What I work with

  • Street-view panoramas — 360° street-level imagery.
  • UAV imagery & LiDAR point clouds — aerial captures used to measure buildings, roads, and canopy structure.
  • GIS layers — road network and spatial data tying everything back to specific stretches of street.
